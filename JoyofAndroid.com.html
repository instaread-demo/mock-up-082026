Screen Mirroring Not Working? Here are the Proven Fixes

Whether you’re streaming a movie, sharing photos, or presenting slides, screen mirroring issues can quickly become frustrating. Users often wonder why screen mirroring is not working on their TV, but the cause is usually a compatibility, connection, or device-setting issue.

In this blog, we’ll cover the most common screen mirroring problems and practical fixes that work in 2026. Before exploring the solutions, let’s first understand what screen mirroring is and why it may fail.

What is Screen Mirroring and Why Does It Sometimes Fail?

Screen mirroring displays the content from your smartphone, tablet, or laptop on a larger screen, such as a smart TV or monitor. It is useful for watching videos, presenting slides, sharing photos, and playing games. However, screen mirroring may not work when the devices use different connection technologies, network settings, or software versions. 

One common reason for screen mirroring problems is that devices may support different wireless display technologies. The most widely used options include: 

  • Miracast: A wireless display standard supported by many Windows computers, smart TVs, and selected Android devices.
  • AirPlay: Apple’s wireless streaming and screen-mirroring technology for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and compatible smart TVs.
  • Google Cast: A casting technology used by Chromecast devices, Google TV products, and compatible apps.
  • Smart View: Samsung’s built-in feature for connecting supported Galaxy devices to compatible TVs and displays.

These compatibility differences can prevent devices from detecting or connecting correctly. 

The good news is that most of these problems have simple solutions. Let’s go through the most common screen mirroring problems and how to fix screen mirroring step by step.

Common Screen Mirroring Problems and How to Fix Them

The following are the most common screen mirroring problems and their fixes.

  1. Phone compatibility problems
  2. TV compatibility problems
  3. TV not showing up as an option
  4. Screen mirroring stuck on connection
  5. Nothing works

1. Phone Compatibility Problems

Not every smartphone supports screen mirroring natively. This compatibility issue is one reason screen mirroring may not work or the TV may not appear as an available device. 

  • Check your phone’s manual or specs online to confirm whether it supports mirroring.
  • If native mirroring is unavailable, try a third-party app such as AirDroid Cast to connect compatible devices through an alternative screen-sharing method. 
  • As a more reliable alternative, you can always use an HDMI adapter to connect directly to your TV.

2. TV Compatibility Problems

TV compatibility problems-Screen Mirroring Problems

Even if your phone supports mirroring, your TV might not. This is a common reason for screen mirroring not working, especially with older TV models. 

How to fix it:

  • Most newer smart TVs already have mirroring built in. For older models, add a device like Chromecast, Roku, or Fire Stick.
  • Double-check your screen mirroring settings on the TV, as some models require you to enable the option manually.
  • If your TV does not support wireless mirroring, use a compatible HDMI cable or adapter for a more reliable wired connection. 

3. TV Not Showing up as an Option

TV is not showing up as an option-Screen Mirroring Problems

If your phone and TV are compatible but the TV does not appear as an available option, the issue may be caused by network settings, blocked device discovery, or disabled casting or mirroring features. 

How to fix it:

  • Make sure your phone and TV are connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Also check that the phone has local-network or nearby-device permission, where required. 
  • Restart your router, TV, and phone to reset the connection.
  • On certain TVs, you may need to open the mirroring input or press the dedicated remote button.
  • If detection issues continue, third-party apps such as ApowerMirror or LetsView can help by scanning and connecting more reliably.
  • For Chromecast users, the Google Home app is another excellent option.

4. Screen Mirroring Stuck on Connecting

A screen mirroring connection may remain stuck on “Connecting” or display a black screen because of an unstable Wi-Fi connection, outdated software, or a temporary device error. 

How to fix it:

  • Turn your phone’s Wi-Fi off and back on, then reconnect both devices to the same network. 
  • Power cycle your TV by unplugging it for 2–3 minutes.
  • Move your phone closer to the TV to avoid weak Wi-Fi signals.
  • If Smart View is not working, confirm that the Samsung phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, then install any available software updates on both devices. 
  • As a last resort, switch to a third-party mirroring app such as AirDroid Cast.

5. Nothing Works? Try an Alternative Casting Method

If you have tried all the common fixes and your screen mirroring is not working until then, the issue may be related to device compatibility. Some phones, TVs, and operating systems use different casting standards, so they may not connect reliably even when both devices support screen mirroring.

In that case, consider using an external streaming device such as Chromecast, Fire TV Stick, Roku, or Apple TV. These devices connect through your TV’s HDMI port and provide their own casting or screen-sharing options. However, compatibility still depends on your phone, apps, and the streaming device’s supported protocols.

You can also try a wired HDMI adapter when you need a more stable connection for presentations, videos, or gaming. Before purchasing any adapter or streaming device, confirm that it supports your phone, TV, and preferred mirroring method.

Frequently Asked Questions on Screen Mirroring Problems

1. What is screen mirroring?

Screen mirroring is a feature that displays your phone, tablet, or laptop screen on a compatible TV in real time. It can be used to share photos, videos, presentations, and apps on a larger screen.

2. Why is screen mirroring not working?

Screen mirroring may not work if both devices are connected to different Wi-Fi networks, mirroring permissions are disabled, or the software is outdated. Restart both devices, check the connection settings, and install any available updates.

3. Why is screen mirroring lagging or disconnecting?

Screen mirroring may lag or disconnect because of weak Wi-Fi, network congestion, or excessive distance between the devices and router. Move the devices closer, close bandwidth-heavy apps, and restart your router.

4. How do I use screen mirroring on a Samsung TV?

To use screen mirroring on a Samsung TV, connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network and open Smart View on your Samsung phone. Select your TV and approve the connection request when it appears.

5. How do I use screen mirroring on an iPhone?

For screen mirroring on an iPhone, connect the iPhone to an AirPlay-compatible TV on the same Wi-Fi network. Open Control Centre, tap Screen Mirroring and select the TV from the list.

Fix Screen Mirroring Problems With the Right Method

Screen mirroring problems can result from Wi-Fi issues, outdated software, unsupported devices, or incompatible casting standards. Start with the basic fixes, confirm that both devices support the same mirroring method, and try an alternative such as Chromecast, AirPlay, Miracast, or an HDMI cable if the built-in option still fails. If the issue started after an Android update, check our guide on Android update problems, as updates can sometimes affect screen mirroring settings, permissions, or device compatibility.

 Choosing the method that matches your phone, TV, and operating system is often the fastest way to get screen mirroring working again.
