If your phone won’t charge, don’t panic. Whether you own an iPhone or an Android device, charging problems are often caused by something simple rather than an expensive hardware failure. Before paying for a repair, work through these proven fixes. If your phone also gets unusually warm while charging, read our guide on Why Phones Get Hot for additional troubleshooting.

1. Check the Charging Cable

Charging cables wear out more often than people realize.

Look for:

  • Frayed ends
  • Bent connectors
  • Loose USB plugs
  • Exposed wires

If possible, try another certified charging cable.

2. Try a Different Charger

The charger itself could be the problem.

Test:

  • Another wall adapter
  • Another USB-C charger
  • A different outlet
  • A computer USB port

If another charger works, you’ve found the issue.

3. Clean the Charging Port

Pocket lint is one of the most common causes of charging problems.

Use:

  • A wooden toothpick
  • A soft anti-static brush
  • Compressed air (gentle bursts)

Avoid metal objects that could damage the charging pins.

4. Restart Your Phone

Software glitches can interrupt charging.

Restart the device and reconnect the charger after it powers back on.

5. Remove Thick Phone Cases

Some rugged cases prevent charging cables from seating completely.

Remove the case and reconnect the cable.

6. Let the Battery Recover

If your battery has been completely drained, it may stay black for several minutes.

Leave it connected to power for at least 30 minutes before assuming it’s dead.

7. Check for Moisture Warnings

Modern phones detect moisture inside the charging port.

If you receive a moisture alert:

  • Disconnect the charger
  • Let the phone dry naturally
  • Avoid using heat or rice

8. Try Wireless Charging

If your phone supports wireless charging and it works normally, the charging port may be damaged rather than the battery.

9. Install Software Updates

Charging bugs occasionally appear after major software releases.

Install any available updates before assuming it’s a hardware issue.

10. Check Battery Health

Older batteries eventually wear out.

On iPhone:

Settings → Battery → Battery Health & Charging

On Android:

Many manufacturers include battery diagnostics within Settings or their support apps.

If battery health is very low, replacement may be the best solution.

When It’s Time for Repair

You may need professional service if:

  • The charging port feels loose
  • The phone only charges at one angle
  • It overheats while charging
  • It stops charging completely with multiple chargers
  • The battery drains unusually fast

Final Thoughts

Most charging problems are caused by worn cables, dirty charging ports, or faulty chargers rather than expensive hardware failures. Taking a few minutes to work through these steps can often save you the cost of an unnecessary repair.
