Victoria hires new deputy city manager with 30 years of municipal experience

Published 3:20 pm Tuesday, August 4, 2026

By Advocate Staff

Deputy City Manager June Ellis (City of Victoria)

The City of Victoria has hired June Ellis as deputy city manager, where he will work under City Manager Jesús A. Garza and oversee several key departments, including public safety and human resources.

The city announced Ellis’ hiring Tuesday, highlighting his 30 years of municipal experience in executive leadership, financial management, strategic planning and organizational development.

“As Victoria continues to grow, we have an opportunity to improve targeted support for public safety, economic development and other critical areas,” Garza said. “June’s experience will be a great complement to our existing executive leadership team, and I am confident that we are now better positioned for success in the coming years.”

As deputy city manager, Ellis will oversee the Victoria Police Department, Victoria Fire Department, Municipal Court and Human Resources Department. He also will assist with City Council relations and support Garza in managing the city’s daily operations.

Ellis most recently served as assistant city manager for the City of Beaumont. During his career, he has led initiatives focused on strategic planning, economic development, financial management and organizational improvement.

While with Beaumont, Ellis helped develop the city’s first 15-year Strategic Plan, created its first Community Investment Plan and negotiated the city’s firefighter union contract.

    • Previously, Ellis served as finance director for the City of Buda, where he helped lead financial management efforts, and worked with the City of Pearland, where he designed a new Performance Measures Reporting System.

    Ellis said he was drawn to Victoria by its leadership, collaborative culture and continued growth as a regional hub.

    “I am excited to join the City of Victoria and contribute to the important work already underway,” Ellis said. “Victoria continues to experience growth and opportunity, and I look forward to working alongside City leadership, employees and the community to advance initiatives that enhance quality of life and position the City for long-term success.”

    Ellis earned a bachelor’s degree in political science from Texas Southern University and a master’s degree in political science from Wayne State University.

    He is a Credentialed Manager Candidate through the International City/County Management Association and has completed professional certifications through the Texas State University Certified Public Manager Program, the University of Oklahoma Economic Development Institute, the North Central Texas Council of Governments and the Government Finance Officers Association of Texas.
