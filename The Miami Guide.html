Miami Spice is Miami’s annual restaurant month — running throughout August and September — when more than 100 of the city’s best restaurants offer specially curated three-course lunch and dinner menus at fixed, affordable prices. Started by the Greater Miami Convention & Visitors Bureau, the program gives locals and visitors a chance to try high-end restaurants at a fraction of the regular cost. Below, find every participating restaurant we’re tracking this year, organized by neighborhood, plus our picks for new spots and the best lunch and brunch deals.

Miami Guide Take

Miami Spice is a city-wide dining promotion held every August and September, where participating restaurants offer prix-fixe lunch and dinner menus — typically $40–$65 per person — featuring dishes from their regular menu at a discounted, fixed price. It’s one of the best times of year to eat at Miami’s top restaurants without the usual price tag.

FAQ

When is Miami Spice 2026?

Miami Spice runs throughout August and September.

How much does a Miami Spice meal cost?

Prices typically range from $40–$65 per person for a three-course menu, with a few premium experiences priced higher.

Do I need a reservation for Miami Spice?

Yes — reservations are strongly recommended, especially at popular spots, since tables book up fast once the season starts.

Are Miami Spice menus available every day of the week?

It varies by restaurant. Most offer Miami Spice menus on select days (commonly Sunday–Thursday for dinner), while some offer it daily. Check the “Days offered” listed for each restaurant above.

Can I get Miami Spice for lunch and brunch, not just dinner?

Yes — many restaurants offer discounted lunch and brunch menus during Miami Spice. See our Best for Lunch & Brunch section above.
