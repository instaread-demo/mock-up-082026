Looking for easy back to school teacher gifts? These simple welcome back teacher gift ideas are a thoughtful way to start the school year off right. From candy jars and coffee gift cards to pencils, markers, notebooks, and classroom supplies, these gifts are cute, useful, and easy for kids to take to school.

Back to school gifts for teachers do not need to be big or expensive. A printable tag, a small treat, or a useful classroom supply can make meet the teacher night, open house, teacher work week, or the first day of school feel extra special.

For even more school-year ideas, visit the Back to School hub for printables, coloring pages, lunch box notes, jokes, teacher gifts, and first day of school fun. You can also find more ways to celebrate teachers all year long with these teacher appreciation ideas.

Best Back to School Teacher Gift Ideas

A back-to-school teacher gift doesn’t have to be complicated. The point is to acknowledge the occasion and make the beginning of the school year feel special. These small gifts are perfect for meet the teacher night, back to school open house, the first day of school, PTA or PTO teacher gifts, staff welcome back gifts, or a simple classroom surprise.

Sweets for the Sweet Teacher

Many teachers keep candy on their desks to reward students, so help keep them stocked with a sweet first week of school surprise.

This Year Is Going to Be Sweet Tag

This “This Year is Going to be Sweet” tag is a cute way to dress up a jar of candy for the first week of school.

How Sweet It Is to Be Taught by You

A jar filled with candy and tied with a pretty bow makes an easy welcome back teacher gift. It is simple, cheerful, and perfect for meet the teacher night or the first day of school.

Print the label on sticker paper and place it on the front of a jar so the teacher can be reminded what a great teacher they are and also have a treat ready for their class.

Caffeinated Teachers Are Happy Teachers

Help teachers get back into the school routine with a Starbucks gift card or a local coffee shop gift card. These free printable gift card holders make DIY teacher gifts quick and easy.

Coffee Shop Gift Card Gift Printables

A coffee shop gift card is one of the easiest back to school gifts for teachers. Print the card, tuck in the gift card, and your child has a useful and thoughtful gift ready to go.

Back to School Shopping Isn’t Just for Students

Don’t forget back to school shopping isn’t just for kids. Teachers spend an incredible amount of money on their classrooms. How about welcoming a teacher back to school with a gift card so they can buy school supplies like extra dry-erase markers and other goodies?

Back to School Shopping Printable Gift Card Holder

This printable gift card holder is a practical way to give a teacher a little help with classroom supplies. It works well with an Amazon gift card, Target gift card, office supply store gift card, or any place your teacher loves to shop.

Do You Have a Punny Teacher This Year?

Give the teacher a sweet treat while welcoming her back to school with one of these four free printable tags. Place some candy in a pretty mason jar, attach a tag, and you have an easy back-to-school teacher gift.

Candy Pun Gift Tags for Teachers

Candy Pun Gift Tags for Teachers

Candy jars make easy back to school gifts for teachers. Fill a jar with candy, tie on one of these free printable candy pun tags, and you have a sweet welcome back teacher gift for meet the teacher night or the first day of school.

Back to School Teacher Gift Tags

A cute printable tag makes even a simple school supply feel like a special back to school teacher gift. Add a tag to markers, highlighters, sticky notes, hand sanitizer, candy, coffee, or a gift card, and you have an easy welcome back gift ready in minutes.

I also have a set of Back to School Teacher Gift Tags with over 50 clever sayings and gift ideas, perfect for first day of school gifts, teacher work week, and last-minute teacher appreciation gifts. These back-to-school teacher gift tags will also keep you prepared for last-minute teacher gifts all year.

Gifting School Supplies Is Always Helpful

Teachers can always use extra supplies. Pencils, markers, highlighters, Sharpies, glue sticks, sticky notes, notebooks, and dry erase markers all make useful back to school teacher gifts.

Add a printable tag and a ribbon, and even a simple school supply becomes a thoughtful welcome back gift.

My Future Is Sharp With You as My Teacher

Delight your child’s new teacher with Sharpies! This simple gift of Sharpies bound with twine gets top marks.

Sharpies are useful in almost every classroom, and this gift is easy for kids to carry to school.

Mark My Words It’s Going to Be a Great Year

This simple gift of Sharpies bound with twine gets top marks!

Post-it Note Gift

Every teacher can use more Post-it notes. It is super simple to print on the top sheet to make a cute gift that is also useful.

A Post-it note gift is small, inexpensive, and perfect for the first week of school.

Have a Bright School Year

School supplies make cute gift ideas! Make your favorite teacher’s day with this fun teacher gift using markers or highlighters.

This is a great choice for teachers who love colorful supplies and bright classroom tools.

Let’s Start the Year “Write”

These first day of school teacher gifts are sure to start the year off right. Pencils make a great teacher gift, especially when tied with a cute ribbon.

This is an affordable idea if you need gifts for more than one teacher.

Notebook Teacher Gift

This idea is easy for your child to take to school, and the teacher will appreciate an extra notebook.

A sweet note inside offering to help through the school year, and maybe a gift card to buy needed items for the class, would make this gift extra special.

Glue Stick Wreath

A teacher can never have enough glue sticks for her classroom! This glue stick wreath is clever, useful, and fun for back to school.

It is a great teacher gift idea for elementary classrooms where glue sticks are always needed.

Sweet Scents

A fresh scent can help make the classroom feel warm and welcoming for the new school year.

Scentsational Teacher Gift

This tag is perfect for a room spray, plug-in air freshener, essential oil diffuser, candle warmer, scented lotion, or hand soap.

If I Can Lend You a Hand Tag

Use the “If I Can Lend You a Hand” tag with hand soap, hand sanitizer, lotion, or another useful classroom item. It is a clever way to share your contact information and let your child’s teacher know you are happy to help during the school year.

EOS always makes for a fun gift, and this printable card makes it perfect for back to school gift ideas.

It is small, cute, and easy to tuck into a teacher gift bag.

EOS Teacher Gift

Pair an EOS lip balm with the printable card for an easy teacher survival gift. It is a cute way to say welcome back without spending much.

Apple for the Teacher Gift

This is a sweet handmade teacher gift idea for the first day of school.

This ruler planter is a fun school-themed teacher gift and makes a cute classroom decoration.

Printable teacher appreciation gift tags are one of the easiest ways to make a small gift feel special.

Use them on pencil jars, candy jars, notebooks, school supplies, gift cards, or classroom treats. Keep a few printed and ready so you can make a quick teacher gift whenever you need one.

What Do Teachers Really Want for Back to School?

The best back to school teacher gifts are usually simple and useful. Gift cards, classroom supplies, favorite snacks or drinks, sticky notes, dry erase markers, hand sanitizer, and handwritten notes are all thoughtful choices.

The easiest way to give your teacher the perfect gift is to know what they actually like. Print the teacher favorite things questionnaire below at the beginning of the year and keep it handy for birthdays, holidays, Teacher Appreciation Week, and end-of-year gifts.

Teacher’s Favorite Things Printable

A teacher’s favorite things printable helps you learn your teacher’s favorite treats, drinks, restaurants, stores, colors, and classroom needs. It is a simple way to make every teacher gift feel more personal and useful all year long.

Back to School Teacher Gift FAQs

What is a good back to school teacher gift?

A good back to school teacher gift is simple, useful, and thoughtful. Candy jars, coffee gift cards, pencils, markers, sticky notes, hand sanitizer, classroom supplies, and printable gift tags all make easy welcome back gifts.

Do you have to give a teacher a gift on the first day of school?

No, a teacher gift is never required. It is simply a kind way to welcome your child’s teacher back to school and show appreciation at the start of the year.
