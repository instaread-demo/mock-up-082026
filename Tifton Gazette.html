Decades-old Tifton businesses to close as owners retire

Published 6:08 pm Monday, August 3, 2026

By Davis Cobb

Andy and Pam Ellis thanked the community for supporting their business for over forty years, but felt it was time for them to begin a new chapter in their retirement and with their family. - Photo courtesy of Rehab Services of Tifton

TIFTON — Rehabilitation Services of Tifton and the Tifton Fitness and Wellness Center will be closing next month.

After forty-three years of operation and service to the community, owners Andy and Pam Ellis announced they would be closing the doors of their businesses Sep. 4 to focus on spending more time with their family and enjoying their retirement.

The closure will include the couple’s two Tifton establishments, as well as Rehabilitation Services of Coffee, located in Douglas.

The Ellis’s first established Rehabilitation Services of Tifton in 1983, offering physical, occupational, and speech therapy as well as athletic training. The Fitness and Wellness Center followed in 1993 when the clinic relocated to a new facility, expanding services to include a professionally supervised fitness center.

Outside of their business, the pair remained committed to the Tiftarea community, offering athletic training and free physicals to students of local schools, organizing the Sports Medicine Seminar for twenty years to provide education on sports-related injuries to professionals around the Southeast, and raising money for charity across twenty-one years through the Sweetheart Run.

Andy Ellis expressed he and his wife felt fortunate to have done their work for as long as they did, but determined it was time to move on to a new chapter of their lives and thanked the community for the years of support.

“Your trust has meant more to us than words can express, and we are forever grateful to have served this community,” he said. “It has truly been an honor and a privilege to care for you and your families.”

While the businesses will permanently close Sep. 4, the owners reported that the administrative offices will remain open through Sep. 30 and that they will continue to monitor their email, rehaboftifton@yahoo.com, afterwards. Patients may also request a copy of their medical records prior to Sep. 30 via the aforementioned email or by calling (229)-386-5200.
