It was a good run, and Sweet Times Bakery husband-and-wife owners Kim and Jeff Roberge met a lot of great people — and their kids and in some cases, their kids, too.

But it was time for a change.

Sweet Times Bakery, a popular cafe for baked goodies and lunches in Victor, will be closing its doors after 36 “wonderful years” of business.
