    Called the “best rock and roll violinist of a generation,” the performer known as Blue Violin returns to the Keweenaw for a show in L’Anse Saturday. “Thunderstruck: A Classic Rock Tribute,” will be performed at 7:30 Saturday at Linden Creek Farm in L’Anse.

    Once again, the show promises an Immersive Candlelit Journey Through Iconic Rock Anthems, hailed as “Rock and roll meets classical music in a gorgeously crafted display of music."

    According to his website, “With a presence as bold as his sound, Blue Violin commands grand concert halls and intimate candlelit venues alike, moving dynamically across the stage while weaving immersive multimedia into every performance.

    His style blurs the lines between classical mastery and rock-and-roll fire, captivating audiences around the world.

    A genre-bending soloist, Blue Violin has brought his high-energy performances to sold-out audiences across Australia, Asia, the United Kingdom, Canada, and throughout the United States.

    With a signature style that blends live loops, soaring melodies, and virtuosic technique, he transforms a single violin into an entire orchestra--whether reimagining Thunderstruck by AC/DC or delivering an epic, multi-layered version of Free Bird.

    Ticket information is available at blulel-violin.com/thunderstruck-L’anse

    Linden Creek Farm is located at 16801 Dynamite Hill Rd. L’Anse.
